Project Overview

EcoCare was engaged to execute a comprehensive deep clean, high-filtration carpet extraction, and hygienic restroom descaling for a multi-story corporate commercial facility in Accra prior to executive compliance auditing. The facility operates daily with high foot traffic, executive meeting rooms, and dense workstation areas requiring spotless presentation.

Our operations team mobilized specialized industrial cleaning equipment, low-moisture carpet extractors, and plant-derived hospital-grade disinfectants to thoroughly transform the facility overnight without disrupting normal working hours.

Key Challenges

The engagement presented three critical operational requirements:

  • Strict Overnight Window: All work completed within 12 hours (7:00 PM - 7:00 AM) before morning staff arrival.
  • Zero Odor Impact: Eco-friendly, low-VOC detergents to avoid respiratory sensitivity among corporate staff.
  • Rapid Drying: Wall-to-wall fitted carpeting across conference suites dry and walk-ready by 7:30 AM.

Solutions & Execution

EcoCare deployed a supervised multi-specialist crew equipped with industrial extraction units and high-velocity air movers:

  • Deep hot-water & encapsulation carpet restoration across 450 sqm of office floor space.
  • Sanitary washroom descaling and antibacterial fogging.
  • High-touch surface disinfection across workstations, glass partitions & elevators.
  • Pre-opening 50-point walkthrough inspection with the client facility manager at 6:45 AM.
