Doorstep Mobile Car Detailing, Exterior Wash & Interior Care

Navigating Accra traffic and daily commutes exposes vehicles to relentless red dust, road film, grease, and tropical weather on the outside, and food spills, body oils, and embedded soil on the inside. Over time, road grime dulls exterior clear coats and wheel rims, while dust deeply penetrates fabric seat cushions and floor carpeting.

EcoCare Cleaning Services Limited brings comprehensive mobile automotive care directly to your driveway, home compound, or corporate office parking lot across Greater Accra. As confirmed in our official promotional portfolio, our mobile crew delivers complete Car Detailing and Cleaning covering both exterior wash & detailing and interior upholstery extraction using calibrated extraction wands, scratch-free microfiber wash mitts, and eco-friendly foaming detergents.

Exterior Foam Wash & Detailing

Scratch-free hand foam bath, high-pressure rinse, wheel rim brake-dust removal, tire gloss dressing, and exterior glass crystal wipe.

Interior Seat & Carpet Extraction

Pressurized shampooing and high-velocity vacuum recovery lift deep dirt, sweat, and beverage stains from seats, mats, and carpets.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean cars at my home or office?
Yes. EcoCare provides fully mobile car interior cleaning. We come directly to your residence, compound, or company office parking space across Greater Accra. We only require access to an electrical outlet and a domestic water tap to operate our equipment.
How long do fabric seats take to dry after extraction?
Our high-velocity vacuum recovery extracts over 85% of applied moisture during shampooing. In Accra's warm weather with doors or windows slightly cracked open for airflow, seats dry completely in approximately 2 to 4 hours.
Does this service include both exterior wash and interior cleaning?
Yes! As confirmed in our official service portfolio, EcoCare provides comprehensive mobile car detailing covering complete exterior hand foam washing, wheel rim descaling, and tire dressing, as well as intensive interior seat shampooing, leather conditioning, carpet extraction, and dashboard detailing directly at your location.
How do you clean leather vehicle seats?
Leather seats are treated with specialized pH-balanced leather cleaners using ultra-soft microfiber brushes to lift dirt from grain and stitching without scratching. We then apply an enriching leather conditioner to protect against sun cracking and maintain suppleness.
