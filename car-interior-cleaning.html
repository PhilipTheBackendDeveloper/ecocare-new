Doorstep Mobile Car Interior Cleaning & Upholstery Care

Navigating Accra traffic and daily commutes exposes vehicle interiors to relentless red dust, road grime, food spills, perspiration, and persistent odors. Over time, dust deeply penetrates fabric seat cushions and floor carpeting, while leather surfaces accumulate body oils and lose suppleness under hot weather conditions.

EcoCare Cleaning Services Limited brings specialized interior extraction equipment directly to your driveway, home compound, or corporate office parking lot. Our mobile team focuses exclusively on thorough, hygienic **interior cleaning**—restoring fabric seats, treating leather, extracting floor carpets, wiping down dashboards, and detailing door panels.

Deep Fabric & Carpet Extraction

Pressurized shampooing and high-velocity vacuum extraction lifts ingrained dirt, dust, and beverage stains from seats and floor mats.

Leather & Console Sanitization

Gentle pH-balanced leather conditioning plus detailed wipe-down of dashboard, AC vents, steering wheel, and door cards.

What Our Interior Service Covers

Our mobile interior deep cleaning addresses all high-contact passenger areas:

  • Fabric Seats Deep Shampooing & Stain Spotting
  • Leather Seats Gentle Cleansing & Conditioning Balm
  • Floor Carpet & Rubber Mat Extraction
  • Dashboard, Steering Column & Instrument Panel Detailing
  • AC Vent Grille Dusting & Crevice Vacuuming
  • Door Trims, Armrests & Glove Box Detailing
  • Trunk / Boot Vacuuming & Spare Tire Compartment Clean

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you clean cars at my home or office?
Yes. EcoCare provides fully mobile car interior cleaning. We come directly to your residence, compound, or company office parking space across Greater Accra. We only require access to an electrical outlet and a domestic water tap to operate our equipment.
How long do fabric seats take to dry after extraction?
Our high-velocity vacuum recovery extracts over 85% of applied moisture during shampooing. In Accra's warm weather with doors or windows slightly cracked open for airflow, seats dry completely in approximately 2 to 4 hours.
Does this service include exterior body washing or polishing?
This specialized service focuses strictly on **interior hygiene, seat upholstery extraction, and cabin sanitization**. We do not perform exterior paint polishing, ceramic coating, underbody degreasing, or engine mechanical repairs.
How do you clean leather vehicle seats?
Leather seats are treated with specialized pH-balanced leather cleaners using ultra-soft microfiber brushes to lift dirt from grain and stitching without scratching. We then apply an enriching leather conditioner to protect against sun cracking and maintain suppleness.
