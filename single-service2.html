Meticulous Laundry, Fabric Preservation & Ironing Care

Eco-Laundry is EcoCare's dedicated garment and textile care branch headquartered at Achimota Mile 7, Accra. We understand that your clothing and household linens represent personal comfort, professional image, and financial investment. Our laundry facility combines high-capacity commercial washing technology with delicate artisanal finishing to keep every item fresh, sanitized, and impeccably pressed.

Whether you require weekly family wash-and-fold services, crisp steam pressing for corporate suits and traditional wear, or bulk linen management for guest houses and hospitality venues, Eco-Laundry guarantees prompt turnaround and fabric-safe cleaning agents.

Delicate Fabric Protection

Color sorting, fabric-specific temperature cycles, and pH-neutral detergents preserve fiber longevity.

Doorstep Pickup & Delivery

Convenient scheduled collection and delivery routes across Greater Accra (Achimota, East Legon, Airport, Spintex).

Eco-Laundry Core Services

Comprehensive textile and wardrobe care delivered with speed and care:

  • Wash, Dry & Fold (Everyday casuals & work wear)
  • Steam Ironing & Hand-Finishing
  • Delicate Silks & Ceremonial Attire Care
  • Heavy Duvets, Comforters & Blankets
  • Curtain Drapery Sanitization

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard turnaround time for laundry?
Standard wash, dry, and fold orders are ready within 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day or 24-hour expedited services are available upon request when arranged during morning drop-off.
Can EcoCare pick up and deliver my clothes?
Yes! We provide scheduled pickup and delivery across Greater Accra. Simply call our customer care lines or request a pickup through our online booking form.
