Detailed Dust Removal & Precision Surface Cleaning in Accra

Living and working in Greater Accra presents persistent environmental challenges with airborne road dust, nearby building construction particles, and heavy dry season Harmattan haze. Standard surface sweeping or dry feather dusting simply kicks fine particulates into the air, allowing them to resettle onto delicate electronics, furniture crevices, ledges, and HVAC vents.

EcoCare Professional Dusting Services provides targeted, high-precision dust remediation for residential estates, executive corporate offices, embassies, archives, and renovated facilities. Utilizing high-density electrostatic microfiber capture materials, telescopic high-reach dusting poles, and HEPA-filtered vacuum detailing, our specialists trap and extract dust without scratching sensitive finishes.

Microfiber Static Capture

Positively-charged microfiber technology binds and traps microscopic dust, pollen, and lint instead of dispersing them into your breathing air.

High-Reach & Detail Coverage

Safe reaching of high ceiling cornices, recessed lighting, ceiling fan blades, curtain pelmets, and air return louvers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does professional dusting differ from regular everyday sweeping?
Everyday sweeping or dry dusting usually redistributes lightweight dust into the room's air column, where it settles right back within minutes. EcoCare's technicians use electrostatic microfibers that attract and trap fine dust particles upon contact, combined with detail crevice vacuums to truly remove the material from your premises.
Can you safely clean sensitive office electronics and computers?
Yes. We utilize dry, non-static microfiber cloths and gentle anti-static detailing brushes for computer towers, monitors, cabling clusters, printer perimeters, and server cabinet exteriors without utilizing wet chemicals that could harm delicate hardware.
Is this service helpful during the Harmattan season in Ghana?
Harmattan haze brings intense dry air carrying fine desert sand and airborne particulates into homes and workplaces across Accra. Our detailed dusting service is particularly popular between November and February to remediate seasonal buildup from window sills, fans, and furniture.
Can dusting be booked as a standalone service or combined with general cleaning?
You can book Detailed Dusting either as a dedicated standalone service (e.g. for libraries, archives, or high-end office suites) or combined with EcoClean residential or commercial general cleaning packages.
