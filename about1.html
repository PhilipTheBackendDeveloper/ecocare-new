Know About Us

Delivering Professional Cleaning Across Ghana Since 2014.

EcoCare Cleaning Services Limited was established on September 19, 2014 under the Ghanaian Companies Code (1963). Based in Achimota Mile 7, Accra, we provide comprehensive environmental care, corporate janitorial solutions, EcoFumes pest eradication, Eco-Laundry, and deep carpet extraction.

Led by Chief Executive Officer & Founder Eric Nana Acquah, our mission is captured in our corporate motto: “Protect the Environment, Live Better, Prolong Life.” We combine industrial-grade equipment, rigorous quality oversight, and eco-conscious cleaning detergents to safeguard health, comfort, and workplace productivity.

  • Health & Environmental Protection

    Utilizing non-toxic, eco-safe formulations that eliminate pathogens and dirt without contaminating indoor air or endangering occupants.

  • Vetted, Uniformed & Supervised Teams

    Every assignment is executed by trained personnel working under strict operational supervision and documented quality checklists.

Vetted Professionals

Carefully screened, trustworthy personnel trained in advanced chemical safety, fabric handling, and equipment operation.

Quality Assurance

Supervisory walkthroughs and strict adherence to service-level agreements on every residential or corporate visit.

Transparent Scoping

Accurate, itemized quotations based on room count, property square meters, and frequency in Ghanaian Cedis (GHS).
