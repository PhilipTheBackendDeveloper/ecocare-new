Deep Fabric Sanitization, Leather Care & Furniture Restoration

Sofas, armchairs, dining chairs, and office chairs absorb everyday skin oils, perspiration, beverage spills, dust particles, and pet dander. Over time, these residues dull fabric colors, break down protective coatings, and generate subtle unpleasant odors. Mattresses also harbor microscopic dust mites that exacerbate asthma and allergic reactions.

EcoCare Upholstery Cleaning brings specialized mobile upholstery equipment directly to your premises. Using delicate fiber extraction wands, pH-appropriate fabric detergents, and leather conditioning balms, we restore the visual brilliance and hygienic freshness of your furniture without damaging fragile seams or cushion padding.

Gentle Hand Scrubbing

Customized agitation using soft horsehair brushes and fiber-safe emulsifiers lifts dirt without fiber abrasion.

Mattress Allergen Extraction

High-suction vacuum extraction pulls out dead skin cells, perspiration salts, and dust mite colonies.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does furniture take to dry after cleaning?
Our specialized upholstery tools extract the vast majority of rinse fluid, allowing fabrics to dry completely in 4 to 6 hours with normal airflow.
How do you treat leather furniture?
We use pH-neutral leather cleansers that dissolve embedded soil from leather pores, followed by rich conditioning creams that replenish natural oils and prevent cracking.
Can you clean office task chairs in bulk?
Yes. We regularly handle scheduled weekend or after-hours batch cleaning for corporate workstations and conference rooms across Accra.
How is upholstery pricing calculated?
Pricing is based on furniture size (e.g., 3-seater couch, sectional, armchair, or dining chair) and material (fabric vs. leather). We provide a confirmed total before starting.
