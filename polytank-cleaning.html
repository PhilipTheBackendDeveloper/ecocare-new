Hygienic Polytank & Water Storage Tank Cleaning in Accra

Water storage tanks are vital across Greater Accra for maintaining steady domestic, commercial, and institutional water supplies. Over months of service, airborne dust, municipal line sediment, rust particles, and organic matter settle at the bottom of storage tanks. Warm Ghanaian temperatures can encourage biofilm accumulation and scale on interior tank surfaces.

EcoCare Cleaning Services Limited delivers specialized water storage tank and polytank cleaning across Greater Accra. Our mobile service team handles overhead and ground storage tanks with systematic draining, manual and mechanical scrubbing, non-corrosive eco-friendly washing, thorough high-pressure flushing, and sanitized preparation before refilling.

Sediment & Biofilm Scrubbing

Effective physical removal of accumulated silt, sand, iron sediment, and organic film from tank walls and base.

Eco-Conscious Sanitization

Biodegradable, non-corrosive water-tank washing solutions thoroughly flushed and evacuated before fresh water refill.

Our Systematic Tank Cleaning Protocol

Every tank cleaning follows a clear, safety-first operational procedure:

  • Initial Inspection of Tank Access, Manhole & Condition
  • Isolation & Controlled Drainage of Residual Water
  • High-Pressure & Manual Wall and Floor Sediment Scrubbing
  • Eco-Friendly Sanitizing Wash & Biofilm Breakdown
  • High-Volume Rinsing & Sludge Evacuation via Wet Vacuum
  • Inspection of Lid Seal, Mosquito Mesh & Exterior Wash

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a polytank be cleaned in Ghana?
For domestic residences and commercial facilities, water hygiene authorities generally recommend inspecting and cleaning water storage tanks every 6 to 12 months. Properties in areas with high dust exposure or intermittent pipe-borne water may require more frequent checks.
Do I need to empty all water from the tank before the team arrives?
We recommend timing your cleaning when the tank water level is already low to prevent wasting treated water. Our team can assist with isolating the supply and draining the remaining bottom residue upon arrival.
Are the cleaning agents safe for domestic water storage?
Yes. EcoCare uses non-corrosive, biodegradable, water-safe sanitizing agents that break down biofilm without leaching harmful chemical residues into your tank. All solutions are thoroughly rinsed and vacuumed out before the tank is cleared for refill.
Can you clean elevated overhead rooftop tanks as well as ground tanks?
Yes. We service both ground-level poly tanks and elevated overhead tanks on metal or concrete stands, provided there is safe, secure ladder or rooftop access for our operators and equipment.
Do you provide certified laboratory water testing?
EcoCare focuses on physical sediment extraction, interior wall descaling, and hygienic sanitizing rinses. We do not provide certified laboratory microbiological drinking-water assays unless specifically commissioned as a separate laboratory partnership service.
