Which areas in Ghana does EcoCare cover?
EcoCare provides comprehensive cleaning, fumigation, laundry, carpet, and upholstery services across the Greater Accra Region, including Achimota, Legon, Airport Residential, Cantonments, Dzorwulu, Ridge, Osu, Spintex, Tema, Dansoman, and surrounding districts. Contact us for custom arrangements outside Greater Accra.
How can I request a service quotation or consultation?
You can submit an enquiry through our Online Booking Form, send an email to ecocaregh1on1@gmail.com, or contact our customer support line directly at +233 24 901 4253 / +233 24 380 7100. We will discuss your specific property parameters and provide an itemized quote.
Are cleaning materials, tools, and equipment provided by EcoCare?
Yes. Our cleaning crews arrive on-site fully equipped with professional industrial vacuum extractors, floor polishers, microfiber cleaning kits, ladders, and eco-friendly, biodegradable detergents. You do not need to provide cleaning supplies.
Do I need to be present during the cleaning service?
While you or an authorized representative should be present at the start to grant access and at completion for the walkthrough inspection, you do not need to remain on-site during the entire cleaning process.
How is carpet and upholstery pricing calculated?
Pricing is calculated transparently based on measurable dimensions (square footage or square meters for fitted carpets), piece counts and fabric type (leather vs. fabric seating, sofa size), and the level of deep soiling or spot treatment required. We confirm all costs before beginning work.
How long do carpets and upholstery take to dry?
With our high-velocity extraction equipment, standard upholstery dries within 4 to 6 hours, while hot-water extraction carpets dry within 4 to 8 hours under normal indoor ventilation. In commercial settings utilizing low-moisture encapsulation, carpets are dry within 1 to 2 hours.
Can you arrange laundry pickup and doorstep delivery?
Yes! Eco-Laundry offers scheduled collection and doorstep return across Accra. You can bundle everyday wash-and-fold laundry, delicate dry-cleaned garments, bed linens, and curtains.
What should I do to prepare my premises for pest control (EcoFumes)?
Prior to treatment, please clear food items, kitchen utensils, and pet dishes into sealed containers or refrigerators. Cover aquariums and ensure family members and pets vacate the premises during application. Re-entry is safe 2 to 4 hours post-treatment after airing out the space.
Can I reschedule a booked service?
Yes. If you need to postpone or adjust your appointment, please contact us at least 24 hours in advance via telephone (+233 24 901 4253) or email so we can reassign our field teams to your preferred alternative date without service interruption.
