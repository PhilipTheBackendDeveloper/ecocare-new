Terms of Service & Operational Standards

By booking a service appointment or requesting a quotation from EcoCare Cleaning Services Limited, you agree to the following operational terms and conditions:

1. Quotations, Pricing & Scope Confirmation

All quotes provided via phone, WhatsApp, or our online calculator are preliminary estimates based on client-provided details. Final pricing in Ghanaian Cedis (GHS) is confirmed upon initial on-site walkthrough inspection. Any additional areas or heavy staining identified on-site will be discussed with you before commencing additional work.

2. Access, Utilities & Safety

Clients agree to provide safe access to the premises at the agreed appointment time, including functional running water and electrical power outlets necessary to operate our commercial extraction and scrubbing equipment. For pest control and fumigation (EcoFumes), occupants and pets must vacate the treated premises and observe the re-entry and ventilation period specified on the product label and directed by the on-site operator.

3. Quality Walkthrough & Satisfaction Guarantee

At the conclusion of every cleaning service, our crew supervisor invites you or your designated representative to perform a joint walkthrough inspection. Any areas requiring touch-up are addressed immediately on-site prior to supervisor sign-off.

4. Rescheduling & Cancellations

We understand that unexpected schedule changes happen. Please notify us at least 24 hours in advance if you need to reschedule or postpone your booking so we can reassign field teams without disruption.

5. Contact Information

For questions regarding our service agreements or to discuss corporate commercial contracts, contact us at +233 24 901 4253 or ecocaregh1on1@gmail.com.
