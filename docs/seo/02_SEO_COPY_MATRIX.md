## 1. Editorial Principles & Local Search Context

1. **Human-First Communication:** Copy is written to inform property managers, homeowners, and corporate facility leads in Greater Accra clearly. It avoids mechanical keyword repetition of "Accra Ghana" in every sentence.
2. **Strict Verification Alignment:** Zero ungrounded claims (no "same-day guarantee", no "100% organic chemicals", no "24/7 staff").
3. **Safety-First Pest Control Messaging:** Replaced historical blanket "2–4 hour" vacate assertions with strict adherence to product labels, Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) guidelines, and certified on-site technician instructions.
4. **Natural Local Terminology:** Leverages familiar Ghanaian property terms (e.g., compound cleaning, self-contained apartments, terrazzo scrubbing, water storage tank maintenance, post-construction handover, expatriate tenancy changeovers).
