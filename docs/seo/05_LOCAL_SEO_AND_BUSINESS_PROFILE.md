## 3. Post-Service Ethical Review Generation Workflow

Reviews are one of Google Local's strongest ranking signals (driven by Prominence). However, review gating, paying for reviews, or asking only satisfied customers violates Google's anti-spam policy.

### Ethical 3-Step WhatsApp Feedback Protocol:
1. **Timing:** 2 to 4 hours following completion of cleaning or fumigation.
2. **Standard Message Template:**
   > *"Hello [Client Name], thank you for choosing EcoCare Cleaning Services today! Our dispatch team hopes your space is looking and feeling fresh. We would deeply appreciate your honest feedback to help our crew maintain high standards: [Insert Direct Google Review Shortlink]. Have a wonderful day!"*
3. **Owner Response Protocol:**
   - Respond to every review (positive or negative) within 24–48 hours.
   - For positive reviews: Thank the client and mention the specific service performed (e.g. *"Thank you, Kwame! Our team enjoyed restoring your living room carpet in East Legon.*").
   - For critical reviews: Acknowledge politely, offer immediate phone resolution (+233 24 901 4253), and never argue publicly.
