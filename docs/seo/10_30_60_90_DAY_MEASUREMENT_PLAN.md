## 1. Governance Principles & Realistic Expectations

1. **Honest Attribution:** Search engine optimization is an organic compounding process driven by relevance, distance, and prominence. We do not make fraudulent guarantees of "#1 rank in 7 days" or "instant viral traffic".
2. **Local Customer Focus:** Success is measured by high-intent inquiries from residential and corporate property owners in Greater Accra, not arbitrary vanity impressions from non-serviceable international territories.
3. **Data Integrity:** All conversion tracking measures real human interactions (phone clicks, WhatsApp chats, verified quote submissions).

---

## 2. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Milestone Roadmap

### Phase A: Days 1 – 30 (Technical Indexing, Crawl Foundation & Local Claim)

#### Objectives:
- Ensure 100% of canonical pages (16 URLs) are crawled and indexed by Google and Bing.
- Secure verified Google Business Profile (GBP) and Bing Places listings.
- Verify zero 404 crawl anomalies or redirect loops in Google Search Console.
- Ensure form delivery and WhatsApp dispatch links function flawlessly on mobile.

#### Measurement Checklist & KPIs:
| Metric / Check | Target Indicator | Monitoring Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **Indexed Pages** | 16/16 canonical pages indexed | Google Search Console (Page Indexing report) |
| **Crawl Status** | 0 server errors (5xx), 0 broken links (4xx) | Google Search Console |
| **Sitemap Health** | Status: "Success", 16 discovered URLs | Google Search Console (Sitemaps) |
| **Google Business Profile** | Verified status, 100% NAP consistency | Google Business Profile Dashboard |
| **Branded Query Visibility** | Rank #1 for "EcoCare Cleaning Services Accra" | GSC Performance Report |
| **Core Web Vitals** | Passing Good thresholds (LCP ≤ 2.5s, CLS ≤ 0.1) | GSC Core Web Vitals Report |
| **Direct Conversions** | Baseline tracking established for calls & chats | GA4 Events / WhatsApp Dispatch Desk |

#### Review Actions (Day 30):
- Review Google Search Console "Performance" query report: identify emerging keywords.
- Address any excluded pages in GSC (e.g. "Crawled - currently not indexed").
- Request initial reviews from 3–5 recent satisfied clients via the ethical WhatsApp workflow.

---

### Phase B: Days 31 – 60 (Local Pack Growth & Intent Expansion)

#### Objectives:
- Expand visibility in Google Local 3-Pack for geographic searches in Achimota, East Legon, Cantonments, and Accra Central.
- Monitor click-through rates (CTR) on service pages and optimize title/meta descriptions if CTR is below industry benchmark (2–3%).
- Build high-authority local citations across Ghanaian business directories.

#### Measurement Checklist & KPIs:
| Metric / Check | Target Indicator | Monitoring Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **Local Pack Impressions** | Steady growth (+25% MoM) in Google Maps | GBP Performance Insights |
| **Service Query Clicks** | Initial organic clicks for category terms | GSC Performance Report |
| **Average Organic CTR** | ≥ 2.5% across top 10 impressions | GSC Performance Report |
| **Google Reviews** | 5 – 10 genuine 5-star customer reviews | Google Business Profile |
| **Quote Scoping Volume** | Measurable estimate requests via `/request-quote` | `data/submissions.jsonl` / GA4 |
| **Bounce Rate / Engagement** | Average engagement time ≥ 1m 15s | Google Analytics 4 |

#### Review Actions (Day 60):
- Analyze top-performing service pages (`/cleaning`, `/pest-control`, `/carpet-cleaning`).
- Refine FAQ content on `/faq` based on real incoming customer questions received by dispatch.
- Review and respond to all newly posted Google reviews within 24 hours.

---

### Phase C: Days 61 – 90 (Commercial Authority & Sustained Pipeline)

#### Objectives:
- Establish EcoCare as an authoritative cleaning brand cited by modern search experiences and AI-assisted search tools (Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot).
- Secure consistent inbound weekly corporate janitorial and move-in deep cleaning requests.
- Audit organic search competitors in Accra and identify content gaps.

#### Measurement Checklist & KPIs:
| Metric / Check | Target Indicator | Monitoring Tool |
|---|---|---|
| **Non-Branded Organic Traffic** | Upward compounding organic sessions (+40% vs M1) | Google Analytics 4 |
| **High-Intent Inquiries** | Steady flow of qualified cleaning and pest control leads | Dispatch desk logs |
| **Commercial Contract Leads** | Inquiries for recurring office / facility janitorial | `/request-quote` & Corporate desk |
| **Local Search Ranking** | Top 3 rank for primary neighborhood queries | Local Incognito Search / GBP |
| **Total Verified Reviews** | 15+ verified reviews with owner responses | Google Business Profile |
| **Organic Conversion Rate** | ≥ 3.5% of organic visits taking an action | GA4 Custom Conversion Events |

#### Review Actions (Day 90):
- Comprehensive quarterly technical SEO audit (crawl, canonicals, schema validity).
- Assess whether to publish the first client-approved commercial case study or expansion guide.
- Formulate the next quarter's growth initiatives based on demonstrated customer demand.
