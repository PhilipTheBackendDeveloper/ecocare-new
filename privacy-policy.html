Our Commitment to Your Privacy

EcoCare Cleaning Services Limited ("EcoCare", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our residential and commercial clients across Greater Accra. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you use our website, request a service quote, or engage our cleaning teams.

1. Information We Collect

To provide accurate estimates and dispatch service teams, we collect:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, telephone number(s), and email address.
  • Property & Location Information: Physical premises address, neighborhood/landmark in Accra, property type, room count, and approximate square footage.
  • Service Preferences: Cleaning frequencies, specific stain or pest concerns, and preferred appointment dates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected details strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Generating transparent quotations and scope-of-work agreements in Ghanaian Cedis (GHS).
  • Dispatching vetted field crews and supervisors to your property.
  • Communicating service confirmations, reminders, or arrival notices via telephone, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • Following up after service completion for quality assurance and customer feedback.

3. Data Retention & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Customer contact records are stored securely and accessed only by authorized administrative and dispatch staff. We retain quote records only as long as necessary to fulfill service agreements or comply with statutory accounting requirements in Ghana.
