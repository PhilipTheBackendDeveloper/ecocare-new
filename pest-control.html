Scientific Pest Management & Fumigation Services

EcoFumes is EcoCare's specialized pest eradication and structural fumigation unit. Pests threaten human health, damage valuable physical assets, contaminate food supplies, and violate municipal hygiene regulations. We apply an Integrated Pest Management (IPM) methodology that targets the root causes of infestations rather than just surface symptoms.

Our trained pest management applicators operate in accordance with Ghana Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) public health guidelines, utilizing regulated insecticides, rodenticides, and misting compounds delivered through ultra-low volume (ULV) cold foggers, thermal fogging units, and precise crack-and-crevice baiting guns across Greater Accra and Ghana.

Targeted Species IPM

Custom treatments specifically calibrated for subterranean termites, German cockroaches, rodents, and mosquitoes.

Rigorous Safety Protocol

Clear pre-treatment instructions, strict adherence to product labels, and qualified operator re-entry guidance protect occupants and pets.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a residential property or office be fumigated?
For residential properties, semi-annual (every 6 months) treatments generally provide effective preventive coverage. For commercial food establishments, retail spaces, and warehouses, quarterly maintenance is strongly advised.
Are the chemicals safe for indoor spaces?
We use regulated public health insecticides formulated for structural environments. These are active chemical formulations that must be handled with care: our technicians follow strict product label directions and provide site-specific safety instructions, including food protection, vacating during application, and observing the operator's prescribed re-entry and ventilation interval.
What preparations are required before an EcoFumes visit?
Occupants should cover or store all food items, cooking utensils, and drinking water containers. Humans and domestic pets must vacate during treatment and observe the site-specific re-entry and ventilation interval advised on site by our qualified operator according to the product label.
Can you handle termite infestations in wooden roofs and door frames?
Yes. We perform specialized non-repellent termiticide injections that transfer through the termite colony to eliminate the queen and protect structural wood.
